Ships

One row per hull, identified by its IMO. A hull carries a sequence of registrations — an MMSI, a name and a flag, from a date — and a trip shows the one that was in force on its own date. IMO 8601915 sailed as Amorella 🇫🇮 from 1988 and as Mega Victoria 🇮🇹 from 2022; a crossing in 2008 reads Amorella.
The columns below show each hull as it is now; use Periods to see and edit its history.
Rows marked unregistered are not hulls at all: they are names people have logged that resolve to nothing. They sit in the same table so that a name already held under another spelling is visible before a second row is created for it.
